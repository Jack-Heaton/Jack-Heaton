# Hi, I'm Jack 👋

CTO at planeKrafty · Applied AI engineer · Charleston, SC

I take messy, ambiguous, often regulated problems from first pitch to production, then turn what works into repeatable patterns that scale beyond me.

For 15 years that's meant clinical and financial records in healthcare, a community-currency exchange, and now aviation maintenance. At planeKrafty we run production GenAI on AWS over 134,000+ pages of aircraft records: a cited, agentic research service, an extraction pipeline gated by hand-labeled evals, and a synthetic fleet that doubles as a demo environment and a gold eval set.

### Things I believe after shipping this stuff

- **Evals come before scale.** If you can't say what "correct" looks like, you can't say you're improving.
- **In regulated domains, abstaining beats guessing.** One confident wrong answer costs more trust than ten "I don't know"s.
- **Production GenAI is hard integration work.** High-latency, sometimes-unavailable models living in a low-latency world. I wrap model calls in Lambda durable functions so retries, checkpoints and alerting come built in.
- **The best pattern is one other teams can own** and reuse over and over.
